# 15 — Gate Guide (security staff)

> One page for the people at the car park gate. Print it and keep it at the gate. Last reviewed: 2026-11-22.

## Before your shift

1. Open **ParkSmart** on the gate tablet or phone and sign in with your work account.
2. Go to the **Gate** screen. At the top you see how many people are **checked in** and how many are **still expected**.
3. To use the camera, tap **Use camera**. A handheld scanner works too: it types into the **Scanned code** box.

## When a car arrives

1. Ask the driver to show the **QR code** in their ParkSmart app.
2. Scan it.
3. Read the result:

| You see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **"B1-014 · checked in"** (bay code and name) | Booking confirmed and checked in | Let them in. Tell them the bay |
| **"already checked in"** | They were checked in earlier today | Let them in |
| **"This QR code is not valid. Ask the employee to refresh it."** | Old or copied code. Codes change every day | Ask them to close and reopen the app, then scan again |
| **"This booking is not for today."** | Their booking is for another day | They have no bay today. Ask them to request one in the app (same-day bays may be free) |
| **"…was released because nobody checked in in time"** | They arrived after the check-in deadline and the bay went to someone else | They have no bay today. They can request a same-day bay in the app |

## When scanning does not work

- **Phone dead or no QR code:** find them in **Today's bookings** (search by name or bay) and tap **Check in manually**.
- **Not in the list:** they have no booking today. Do not let them take a bay. They can request a same-day bay in the app.
- **Camera does not start:** use the handheld scanner, or search the list and check in manually.

## Reading the bay map

**Bays today** shows every bay: free, booked (amber), checked in (green), blocked (grey). Blocked bays are reserved, for example for an event. Do not give them out.

## If ParkSmart is down

1. Write down each arrival: time, name, car number and bay used.
2. Call the Facilities Head (admin). They record the check-ins once the app is back.

## Good to know

- Booking early gives no advantage. Everyone's request is scored by the same published rules.
- An employee can tap **Running late** once a week, which gives them 30 more minutes.
- Bays not checked in by the deadline are released automatically and offered to others.
